Master's / MBA in Hospital & Health Care Management

What is the Master's / MBA in Hospital & Health Care Management?

The Master's / MBA in Hospital & Health Care Management is a postgraduate management degree focused on the health sector, including hospitals, clinics, diagnostic centres, health insurance and public health organisations. As an advanced course, study covers a broad range of disciplines such as health care operation management, health care marketing, health informatics, health care law, organisational behaviour and corporate finance. The goal is to combine core management skills with an understanding of how health services are planned, run and improved, so that organisations work well for patients and staff alike.

The programme is open to graduates in any stream from a recognised university, with 60 seats at Salt Lake Campus and the master's degree is awarded by SSU. This Master's / MBA in Kolkata gives students the training and tools needed for a management career in domestic and international health care settings.

The Master's / MBA combines 3 main goals

01
Knowledge & Tools

To provide sound management knowledge and tools for health care settings

02
Strategic Insights

To enrich strategic and operational insight into hospital and health care work

03
Global Management

To prepare participants to manage in domestic and international health care

This specialisation offers a structured management education for graduates who want to build a career in the hospital and health care sector. The Master's / MBA programme in collaboration with SSU, helps graduates prepare for the next step in their career, whether in hospitals, clinics or wider health organisations. Students develop skills across health care operation management, health care marketing, health informatics and health care law, supported by corporate finance, business statistics and accounts for management. Business communication and organisational behaviour help students lead teams in demanding, patient-facing settings.

Students also study research methods in health care, which builds the ability to read evidence, analyse data and apply it to real problems faced by hospitals and wider health organisations. Domestic and international placement support is available to students on completion of the programme.

MBA — Same Programme

The MBA in Hospital & Health Care Management is not a separate course. It is the same two-year Master's / MBA programme described above, open to graduates in any stream from a recognised university. It brings together health care operation management, health care marketing, health informatics and health care law with core business subjects. The aim is to help students understand how health services are organised, financed and improved. This is how a management course focused on health care can shape your career.

The programme has 60 seats. Alongside health care subjects, students study organisational behaviour, corporate finance, business statistics, business communication and accounts for management. Research methods in health care give students a grounding in evidence and analysis. On completion, the master's degree is awarded by SSU, and domestic and international placement support is available for students who want a clear management path in the health care sector.

The Diploma in Hospital Management is a two-year programme for students who have passed or appeared for Class 10 from a recognised Central or State Government board. It introduces the management side of health care — how hospitals are planned, run, marketed and kept compliant — and combines classroom learning with six months of industrial training to prepare students for work in hospitals and wider health care organisations.

Health care is a large and varied field of work, both in India and internationally. This diploma covers management principles, health care operations, health informatics, health care law and business communication, with a certificate awarded by NCVRT on completion. Students also receive domestic and international placement support throughout the programme. The programme has 60 seats.

The healthcare industry covers every organisation involved in keeping people well and treating illness. It includes government and private hospitals, clinics, laboratories, nursing homes and wellness centres, plus health insurance companies and public health departments. Its main purpose is to give patients diagnosis, treatment and care.

Hospital management covers the planning, organisation and day-to-day running of hospitals and health care organisations, so that patients receive safe, timely care and the organisation stays financially sound. It involves managing operations, finances, marketing, information systems, legal compliance and people. Key subject areas include: Hospital Planning, Health Care Operation Management, Health Care Marketing, Health Informatics, Health Care Law, Medical Terminology and Epidemiology. Hospital management is not a theory-only field — it is practical. That is why SBIHM's hospital management courses include six months of industrial training.

The main difference is that hospital management covers all aspects of running a hospital — its departments, patient services, records, finances and staff. In contrast, healthcare management is a broader term covering clinics, laboratories, health insurance, pharmaceuticals and public health. Either route can open a wide range of career opportunities after 12th or graduation.

It depends on where you are now. After Class 10, the two-year Diploma in Hospital Management, with a certificate awarded by NCVRT, is an early route into the field of health care management. After 10+2, the three-year Bachelor in Hospital Management, awarded under MAKAUT, includes six months of industrial training in a multispeciality hospital. Graduates in any stream can take the two-year Master's / MBA in Hospital & Health Care Management, with the master's degree awarded by SSU. Each programme has 60 seats and includes domestic and international placement support. Your counsellor can help you pick the right route for your goals.
